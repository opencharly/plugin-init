# 2026.296.0001 — feat(resolve): carry the container-venue init contract through resolution

## Summary

The `plugin-init` producer leg of the W3.8 cutover (opencharly/charly#1030, spike #1027), on top of opencharly/spec#269.

`candy/plugin-init` is the ONE place an authored `init:` body becomes the RESOLVED envelope every consumer reads. Two changes:

- **The container-venue contract survives resolution.** `resolveInitConfig` is a hand-written field projection, so a new `#Init` field it does not name is silently dropped between the authored vocabulary and every runtime consumer. It now carries `requires_root` / `systemd_mode` / `stop_signal` — the fields that keep a systemd image at root and tell the engine to enable systemd mode — and `extends`, consumed by `ProjectInitConfig`'s merge.
- **The plugin's own CUE schema mirrors core `#Init`** (the documented self-contained reproduction): the new fields, `extends`, and the widened `requires_capability` grammar that admits the `init_system:<name>` hint key (hyphenated init names included).

`params/cue_types_gen.go` is regenerated by the sanctioned pipeline (`charly candy params plugin-init`), never hand-edited; `--check` reports it current.

- `TestResolveInitConfigCarriesContainerRuntimeContract` — the resolved envelope carries `requires_root=true`, `systemd_mode=true`, `stop_signal=SIGRTMIN+3` and the `/sbin/init` entrypoint. Removing the three projection lines fails the test (`RequiresRoot=false SystemdMode=false`, `StopSignal=""`), which is exactly how the omission would otherwise reach a deploy.
